the idea

Most compression tools fight redundancy with brute-force algorithms. Iris takes a different path. It looks at your data first, sorts it by what it sees, then hands it to a codec designed to compress visual similarity.

AV1 does what it was built to do. Your data gets smaller. And it runs on any machine you own.

built on

Iris is made possible by VisionSort, an algorithm that treats sorting as a perception problem through entropy routing.

It utilizes AV1 encoding, an open, royalty-free codec format designed for video transmission over the internet, repurposed here as a general data compressor.
